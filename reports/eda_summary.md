# EDA and unsupervised analysis: summary

Generated by `python -m riskpredictor.eda` from `data/processed/dataset.csv` in 6 s.
Figures are in `figures/eda_*.png`.

## Key numbers

- Commits: **2,788** (2011-02-13 to 2025-08-18); buggy: **610 (21.9%)**, clean: 2,178.
- Imbalance ratio clean : buggy = **3.6 : 1**.
- Time-aware split (oldest 80% / newest 20%): bug rate **23.6%** in train vs **15.1%** in test.
- Features that differ significantly between buggy and clean commits (Mann-Whitney p < 0.05): **8 of 14**.
- Strongest single signal: **Lines added (`la`)**, Cliff's delta = +0.47 (medium).
- PCA: **5 of 14** components are needed for 80% of the variance (PC1 alone: 31%).
- K-Means: chosen **k = 3** (silhouette 0.25); cluster bug rates range from **17.9% to 40.7%**.
